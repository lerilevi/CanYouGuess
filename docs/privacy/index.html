Can You Guess?

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Can You Guess? (“the App”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, and protects your information. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

  • Account information — email, username, and encrypted password.
  • Gameplay data — scores, streaks, badges, answers, and category preferences.
  • Approximate location — general country via IP address (not GPS), used for the “My Country” category and leaderboards.
  • Purchase information — handled by RevenueCat via Apple/Google; we do not store payment details.
  • Advertising data — Google AdMob may collect device identifiers for rewarded ads, subject to your consent.

How We Use Your Information

  • To operate gameplay features.
  • To generate questions using AI.
  • To process purchases.
  • To show rewarded ads if opted in.
  • To maintain account security.

Your Choices and Rights

  • You can update your username, email, or password at any time within the App.
  • You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from your Profile settings.
  • Users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland will see a consent prompt before personalized ads are shown.
  • You can restore previous purchases at any time through the App.

Children’s Privacy

Can You Guess? is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Data Retention

Gameplay data is retained while your account is active. All data associated with your account is permanently deleted upon account deletion.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
